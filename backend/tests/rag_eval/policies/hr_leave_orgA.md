# Acme Corp - Employee Leave Policy (v2)

## 1. Purpose and Definitions
This policy explains how employees of Acme Corp apply for and receive leave. In this policy, "employee" means any full-time staff member, "manager" means the employee's direct line manager, and "working day" means Monday to Friday excluding public holidays. This document was approved by the HR Director and applies to all Acme offices.

## 2. Casual Leave
2.1 Each employee is entitled to 12 days of casual leave per calendar year.
2.2 Casual leave must be applied for at least 7 days in advance using the Leave Application form.
2.3 A single casual leave request may not exceed 5 consecutive working days without written approval from the HR Director.

## 3. Sick Leave
3.1 Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
3.2 A medical certificate from a registered doctor is required when sick leave exceeds 3 consecutive working days.
3.3 Sick leave must be reported to the manager on the first day of absence.

## 4. Carry-over and Encashment
4.1 A maximum of 5 unused casual leave days may be carried over to the next calendar year. Days above this limit lapse on 31 December.
4.2 Unused sick leave cannot be carried over or encashed.

## 5. Maternity and Paternity Leave
5.1 Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave. Paternity leave is 10 working days of paid leave, to be taken within 6 months of the birth.

## 6. Approval
6.1 Leave of up to 5 working days is approved by the manager. Leave longer than 5 working days requires approval from both the manager and the HR Director.
