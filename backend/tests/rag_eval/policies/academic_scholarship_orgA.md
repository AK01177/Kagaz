# Acme University - Scholarship and Fee Policy (v1)

## 1. General
This policy applies to enrolled students of Acme University. "CGPA" means the cumulative grade point average on a 10-point scale. The Registrar administers this policy.

## 2. Merit Scholarship
2.1 A student is eligible for the merit scholarship only if the CGPA is 8.0 or above at the end of the previous semester.
2.2 The scholarship application must include the latest transcript and a fee receipt for the current semester.
2.3 A scholarship is withdrawn if the CGPA falls below 7.5 in any semester.

## 3. Tuition Fees
3.1 Semester fees must be paid before the due date printed on the fee notice.
3.2 A late fee of 2 percent of the outstanding amount is charged for each week of delay.
3.3 Every fee receipt must show the student ID, amount paid, payment date and a unique transaction ID.

## 4. Transcripts
4.1 The first official transcript is free. Each additional copy costs $10.
4.2 Transcript requests are processed within 5 working days.
