# Globex Ltd - Leave Rules (v1)
## 1. Definitions
In these rules "staff member" means any permanent employee of Globex Ltd.
## 2. Casual Leave
2.1 Staff members receive 18 days of casual leave each year, requested at least 3 days in advance.
## 3. Sick Leave
3.1 A medical certificate is required for any sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.
## 4. Carry-over
4.1 Up to 10 unused casual leave days may be carried over to the next year.
