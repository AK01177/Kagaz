# Acme Corp - Expense, Procurement and Invoice Policy (v3)
## 1. Scope and Definitions
This policy governs expense claims, purchase orders and supplier invoices at Acme Corp. "Claimant" means the employee submitting an expense claim, "vendor" means an external supplier, and "business day" means a weekday that is not a public holiday. The Finance Director owns this policy and reviews it annually.
## 2. Expense Claims
2.1 Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the date the expense was incurred. Claims submitted later are rejected.
2.2 An itemised receipt is mandatory for every expense above $25.
2.3 Any single expense claim with a total above $500 requires prior written approval from the department manager. Claims above $2,000 additionally require Finance Director approval.
2.4 Alcohol, personal entertainment and traffic fines are not reimbursable.
2.5 Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $60 per day.
## 3. Purchase Orders
3.1 A purchase order (PO) is required for any purchase above $1,000. Purchases without a PO will not be paid.
3.2 Every PO must state the vendor name, item description, quantity, unit price and delivery date.
## 4. Supplier Invoices
4.1 An invoice must show the invoice number, invoice date, vendor name, tax registration number and total amount.
4.2 Standard payment terms are Net 30 days from the invoice date.
4.3 Invoices received more than 90 days after the invoice date are not accepted for payment.
4.4 The invoice total must match the approved purchase order within a tolerance of 2 percent.
